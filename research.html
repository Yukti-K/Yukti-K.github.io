Research notebook / Yukti Kathuria

Better decisions in complex systems.

My work connects operations management, healthcare operations, and applied analytics. I use statistical, computational, and optimization-based approaches to understand how systems evolve—and how to make better decisions when data, time, and resources are limited.

What I'm trying to understand

02 / In focus

Selected research

2026 · Preprint · Type 2 diabetes / cardiovascular risk

Order-Based Bayesian Network Modeling of Early Detection and Post-Diagnosis Control for Cardiovascular Disease Risk in Type 2 Diabetes

This study develops a temporally structured Bayesian network using EHR data from 9,450 primary-care patients, examining the relationships among early detection of type 2 diabetes, subsequent glycemic control, and cardiovascular risk. The approach highlights differences in individual risk trajectories and how post-diagnosis information can refine risk stratification.

Approach: Bayesian networks, temporally ordered relationships, clinical risk analysis.

With Kristen Miller, Elizabeth Selden, William Gallagher, and Muge Capan.

2024

Healthcare access through clinic allocation

Multi-Criteria Clinic Allocation in Massachusetts to Reduce Access to Healthcare Disparities investigates clinic allocation as an operations and equity problem. Published in the proceedings of the IISE Annual Conference & Expo.

2024

Barriers to physical activity in nursing students

Analysis of Multi-Level Barriers to Physical Activity Among Nursing Students Using Regularized Regression uses a scoring framework and regularized regression to analyze individual, social, and behavioral factors among 163 nursing students.
