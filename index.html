02 / Background

From aerospace to healthcare.

I’m a Ph.D. student in the Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, advised by Muge Capan.

My training began in aerospace engineering, where I earned both my B.S. and M.S. from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Today I bring that systems perspective, together with statistical and computational methods, to healthcare research.

03 / Research

What I'm working on.

My work explores how healthcare data can reveal unequal risks, clarify changing care needs, and support smarter use of limited resources. Recent projects span cardiovascular risk stratification, longitudinal care pathways, and geographic healthcare access.
