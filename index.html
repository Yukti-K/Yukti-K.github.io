I am currently a fourth-year Ph.D. student in Industrial Engineering at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, advised by Muge Capan . My research lies at the intersection of operations research, statistical modeling, machine learning, and healthcare analytics. I develop data-driven and computational methods to understand patient risk, support decision-making under uncertainty, and improve healthcare resource allocation and access to care.

My current research focuses on longitudinal patient risk stratification, particularly understanding how clinical patterns and patient risk profiles evolve over time. Using stochastic and statistical models, I aim to translate complex healthcare data into actionable insights for timely clinical interventions, specialty referral decisions, and efficient and equitable healthcare delivery. I am also interested in how patient population characteristics and geographic factors influence diagnostic outcomes and resource distribution in primary care.

Prior to joining the University of Massachusetts Amherst, I earned my B.S. and M.S. in Aerospace Engineering from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.
