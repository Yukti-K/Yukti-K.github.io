02 / A LITTLE MORE ABOUT ME

Research with a real-world pulse.

I’m a Ph.D. student in the Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, advised by Muge Capan.

My research connects operations research, statistical modeling, machine learning, and healthcare analytics. I develop computational methods to model patient risk, support decisions under uncertainty, and improve access to care.

Before UMass, I earned a B.S. and M.S. in Aerospace Engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
